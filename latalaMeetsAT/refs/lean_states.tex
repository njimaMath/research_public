\section{Executive summary}

The directory \lean{Lemmas} contains 24 Lean files.  Six files contain one
\lean{sorry} each; the other eighteen contain none.  There are no local
\lean{admit} commands or project-specific \lean{axiom} declarations in this
directory.  The six placeholders are in
\lean{GaussianDerivative.lean}, \lean{FreeEnergyDerivative.lean},
\lean{GT/Interpolation.lean}, \lean{FixedDeviation.lean},
\lean{Cavity/System.lean}, and \lean{FreeEnergy.lean}.

The public theorem \lean{SpinGlass.AT.quantitative_strictAT} states the desired
three quantitative conclusions without analytic assumptions.  Its final
assembly is checked, but it calls existence theorems that still contain
\lean{sorry}; hence the public theorem is not Lean-certified.  Moreover,
removing the six placeholders would not by itself make the source a faithful
formalization of the blueprint: the scalar strict-AT estimates and the
analytic Guerra--Talagrand package are still hypotheses, the cavity
interpolation is satisfied by the zero function, and the abstract smart path
has no explicit common Gaussian-coordinate realization.

The status words used below have the following meanings:
\begin{description}
\item[\verified] the stated local theorem has a proof with no placeholder;
\item[\conditional] the Lean deduction is proved, but an intended analytic
input is assumed or omitted from the specification;
\item[\openstate] the file contains a theorem whose proof ends in
\lean{sorry}.
\end{description}

This document follows the mathematical order recorded in
\lean{blueprint_at.aux}.  References such as ``Blueprint (2.7),
\lean{eq:DNprime}'' give both the printed number and the auxiliary-file label.

\section{Mathematical setting and target}

Let $Z$ be standard Gaussian and let $q=q(\beta,h)$ be the
replica-symmetric fixed point
\[
  q=\mathbb E\tanh^2(h+\beta\sqrt q\,Z)
\]
(Blueprint (0.1), \lean{eq:q}).  Put
\[
  r=\mathbb E\tanh^4(h+\beta\sqrt q\,Z),
  \qquad
  a=1-2q+r
    =\mathbb E\operatorname{sech}^4(h+\beta\sqrt q\,Z),
  \qquad
  \alpha=\beta^2a.
\]
On a compact parameter set $K$ in the strict AT region, compactness is used to
obtain
\[
  1-\alpha(\beta,h)\geq\delta_K>0
  \qquad ((\beta,h)\in K)
\]
(Blueprint (0.3)--(0.6), \lean{eq:alpha}--\lean{eq:qcompact}).

Along the replica-symmetric smart path $s\in[0,1]$, write
\[
\begin{aligned}
 A_s&=\mathbb E\langle(R_{12}-q)^2\rangle_s,\\
 B_s&=\mathbb E\langle(R_{12}-q)(R_{13}-q)\rangle_s,\\
 C_s&=\mathbb E\langle(R_{12}-q)(R_{34}-q)\rangle_s,\\
 T_s&=\mathbb E\langle|R_{12}-q|^3\rangle_s.
\end{aligned}
\]
The expectation includes both disorder and Gibbs replicas.  The target theorem
asserts, uniformly for $(\beta,h)\in K$ and $s\in[0,1]$,
\[
  N A_s\leq M_K,
  \qquad
  0\leq p_{\mathrm{RS}}-p_N\leq\frac{M_K}{N},
\]
and
\[
  N(A_s-2B_s+C_s)
  \longrightarrow
  \frac{a(\beta,h)}{1-s\alpha(\beta,h)}.
\]

\section{Proof architecture}

The blueprint proof has four main stages:
\[
  \boxed{\text{RS coercivity}}
  \longrightarrow
  \boxed{\text{preliminary concentration}}
  \longrightarrow
  \boxed{\text{cavity closure}}
  \longrightarrow
  \boxed{\text{quantitative conclusions}}.
\]

\subsection{Replica-symmetric coercivity}

The scalar Parisi equation is solved by an explicit Gaussian semigroup
(Blueprint (1.4)--(1.9), \lean{eq:parisipde}--\lean{eq:RSpathvalue}).  The
strict AT condition should imply the one-sided estimates
\[
  g_s(u)-u\geq\delta_K(q-u)\quad(0\leq u\leq q),
  \qquad
  u-g_s(u)\geq\delta_K(u-q)\quad(q\leq u\leq1).
\]
Lean proves the convolution and Lata\l a kernel identities and derives the
sign and local linear separation of $g_s(u)-u$ from these inequalities.  It
does not yet prove the inequalities themselves.

For two replicas constrained to have overlap $v$, introduce the explicit
Guerra--Talagrand functional $\mathcal P_{\mathrm{GT}}(\lambda,v)$
(Blueprint (1.19)--(1.26), \lean{eq:constrainedZ}--\lean{eq:GTfunctional}).
The intended interpolation and coercivity statements are
\[
  \mathbb E p_{N,s}^{(2)}(v)
  \leq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{GT}}(\lambda,v)
  \leq2p_{\mathrm{RS}}(s)-c_K(v-q)^2.
\]
The explicit finite recursion and the algebraic Taylor minimization are
formalized.  The first inequality, the multiplier Taylor estimates, and the
uniform negative-overlap gap remain open.

\subsection{Preliminary concentration}

Introduce a positive quadratic coupling $\lambda_0$ and decompose the coupled
partition function over the at most $N+1$ attainable overlaps.  GT coercivity
and finite-Gaussian concentration should give the pressure envelope
\[
  p_{N,s}^{(2)}(\lambda_0)
  \leq p_{\mathrm{RS}}(s)+\varepsilon_N,
  \qquad \varepsilon_N\longrightarrow0
\]
(Blueprint (2.4), \lean{eq:pressureenvelope}).  This theorem is not present in
the current \lean{CoupledPressure.lean}; only the algebraic decomposition and
cardinality bound are proved.

Let $D_N(s)=p_{\mathrm{RS}}(s)-p_N(s)$.  Gaussian differentiation yields
\[
  D_N'(s)=\frac{\beta^2}{4}A_s
\]
(Blueprint (2.7), \lean{eq:DNprime}).  Convexity in the coupling and the
pressure envelope give a Gronwall inequality and hence a preliminary
$A_s=o(1)$ estimate (Blueprint (2.8)--(2.9), \lean{eq:gronwallDN} and
\lean{eq:preconcentration}).  A second concentration argument then gives,
for every $\varepsilon>0$,
\[
  \mathbb E\langle
    \mathbf1_{\{|R_{12}-q|\geq\varepsilon\}}
  \rangle_s
  \leq C_{K,\varepsilon}e^{-c_{K,\varepsilon}N}
\]
(Blueprint (2.10), \lean{eq:tail}).  The derivative and fixed-deviation
estimates are both placeholders in Lean.

\subsection{Cavity closure}

For $\mathbf v_s=(A_s,B_s,C_s)^{\mathsf T}$, the last-spin expansion should
produce
\[
  (I-sL)\mathbf v_s
  =\frac1N\boldsymbol\theta+\boldsymbol\rho_s,
  \qquad
  \|\boldsymbol\rho_s\|
  \leq C_K\bigl(N^{-3/2}+T_s\bigr)
\]
(Blueprint (3.1)--(3.5), \lean{eq:cavitysystem}--\lean{eq:rho}).  Lean proves
the coefficient matrix, its exact algebra, and the displayed system after
defining $\boldsymbol\rho_s$ as its residual.  It does not construct the
last-spin interpolation needed to prove the remainder estimate.

The matrix $I-sL$ is uniformly invertible in the strict AT region.  Its
triangularization separates the anomalous and replicon factors, and
\[
  \sup_{(\beta,h)\in K,\,s\in[0,1]}
  \|(I-sL)^{-1}\|<\infty
\]
follows from the AT gap (Blueprint (3.23)--(3.26),
\lean{eq:gammas}--\lean{eq:inverse}).  This matrix analysis is verified.

\subsection{Absorption and conclusions}

Invert the cavity system, split the cubic moment at a small fixed threshold,
and use the exponential tail to absorb the term proportional to $T_s$.  This
gives $N A_s=O_K(1)$ (Blueprint (4.1)--(4.2), \lean{eq:preabsorb} and
\lean{eq:thirdsplit}).  The deterministic absorption is verified conditional
on the tail and remainder estimates.

For the free energy, continuity on $[0,1]$, the product identity $D_N(0)=0$,
and integration of $D_N'=\beta^2A_s/4$ yield
\[
  0\leq D_N(1)\leq C_K/N
\]
(Blueprint (4.3), \lean{eq:freeenergyidentity}).  The calculus is verified;
the continuity and endpoint identity are not.

Finally, concentration and $N A_s=O_K(1)$ imply $N T_s\to0$.  Multiplying the
cavity system by the replicon row $(1,-2,1)$ gives
\[
  N(A_s-2B_s+C_s)
  \longrightarrow
  \frac{a(\beta,h)}{1-s\alpha(\beta,h)}
\]
(Blueprint (4.4)--(4.5), \lean{eq:repliconeq} and
\lean{eq:repliconrho}).  This deduction is verified conditional on the earlier
analytic inputs.

\section{Claims not yet verified in Lean}

The following are the six assertions whose Lean proofs currently contain
\lean{sorry}.  Constants are uniform on the fixed compact strict-AT set.

\begin{openclaim}[Gaussian covariance differentiation]
Let $H_s$ be a centered finite Gaussian process with differentiable covariance
$C_s$.  For every observable $F$ of $n$ replicas,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \frac d{ds}\mathbb E\langle F\rangle_s
 =\frac12\mathbb E\Bigg\langle F\Bigg(&
 \sum_{a,b\leq n}\dot C_s(\sigma^a,\sigma^b)
 -2n\sum_{a\leq n}\dot C_s(\sigma^a,\sigma^{n+1})\\
 &+n(n+1)\dot C_s(\sigma^{n+1},\sigma^{n+2})
 -n\dot C_s(\sigma^{n+1},\sigma^{n+1})\Bigg)\Bigg\rangle_s.
\end{aligned}
\]
This is \lean{quenchedGibbs_deriv_of_covariance_deriv}.
\end{openclaim}

\begin{openclaim}[Smart-path free-energy derivative]
For $0<s<1$,
\[
  p_N'(s)=\frac{\beta^2}{4}
  \left((1-q)^2-A_s\right).
\]
This is \lean{smartPath_freeEnergy_deriv}.
\end{openclaim}

\begin{openclaim}[Two-replica Guerra--Talagrand bound]
For every attainable overlap $v$, every multiplier $\lambda$, and every
$s\in[0,1]$,
\[
  \mathbb E p_{N,s}^{(2)}(v)
  \leq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{GT}}(\beta,h,q,s,\lambda,v).
\]
This is \lean{twoReplica_GT_bound}.
\end{openclaim}

\begin{openclaim}[Fixed-deviation overlap concentration]
For every $\varepsilon>0$, there exist $c,C>0$ such that
\[
  \mathbb E\langle
    \mathbf1_{\{|R_{12}-q|\geq\varepsilon\}}
  \rangle_s
  \leq Ce^{-cN}.
\]
This is \lean{fixedDeviation}.
\end{openclaim}

\begin{openclaim}[Cavity remainder]
There exists $C>0$ such that the residual in the three-dimensional cavity
system satisfies
\[
  \|\boldsymbol\rho_s\|
  \leq C\left(N^{-3/2}+T_s\right).
\]
This is \lean{exists_cavityRemainder_bound}.
\end{openclaim}

\begin{openclaim}[Free-energy endpoint bridge]
The function $D_N(s)=p_{\mathrm{RS}}(s)-p_N(s)$ is continuous on $[0,1]$ and
\[
  D_N(0)=0.
\]
This is \lean{freeEnergy_endpoint_bridge}.
\end{openclaim}

\section{Structural gaps beyond the six placeholders}

\begin{description}
\item[Scalar strict-AT input.] The theorem
\lean{strictAT_sign_of_scalar_linear_bounds} proves the correct sign and local
linear gap for $g_s(u)-u$, but assumes the two inequalities
\[
 g_s(u)-u\geq\delta_K(q-u),\qquad
 u-g_s(u)\geq\delta_K(u-q)
\]
on the left and right of $q$.  No theorem derives them from the AT condition.

\item[GT analytic input.] The structure \lean{GTFunctionalAnalyticData}
assumes a uniform Taylor upper bound
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{GT}}(\lambda,v)
 \leq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{GT}}(0,v)+d(v)\lambda+\frac M2\lambda^2,
 \qquad |d(v)|\geq\delta_K|v-q|,
\]
and a uniform gap for $v\in[-1,-q]$.  Lean proves quadratic coercivity from
these assumptions, but does not construct them from the explicit recursion.

\item[Missing pressure theorem.] The blueprint requires
\[
 p_{N,s}^{(2)}(\lambda_0)
 \leq p_{\mathrm{RS}}(s)+\varepsilon_N,
 \qquad \varepsilon_N\to0.
\]
The current \lean{CoupledPressure.lean} contains the algebra needed to sum
over attainable overlaps but no theorem with this conclusion.

\item[Under-specified cavity interpolation.] The theorem
\lean{exists_cavityInterpolatedAverage} only asks for
\[
 \left|\frac{d^2}{du^2}\nu_{s,u}(F)\right|
 \leq C_K(T_s+N^{-3}),
 \qquad |F|\leq1.
\]
It imposes neither normalization nor $u=0,1$ endpoint identities and is
therefore proved by $\nu_{s,u}(F)\equiv0$.  The intended last-spin
interpolation is not constructed.

\item[Tautological cavity system.] Since
$\boldsymbol\rho_s$ is defined by
\[
 \boldsymbol\rho_s
 =(I-sL)\mathbf v_s-\frac1N\boldsymbol\theta,
\]
the theorem \lean{cavity_system} is an algebraic identity.  Its intended
analytic content lies entirely in the unproved remainder estimate.

\item[Abstract smart-path model.] The structure \lean{RSSmartPathDisorder}
specifies Gaussian marginals, means, and covariances, but not a common
finite-coordinate realization.  An explicit representation, schematically
\[
 H_s\stackrel{\mathrm{law}}=\sqrt{s}\,X+\sqrt{1-s}\,Y,
\]
with the correct covariance factors and independence, would provide a common
foundation for differentiation, concentration, endpoint continuity, and the
last-spin construction.

\item[Scope of the SK wrapper.] The theorem
\lean{quantitative_strictAT_for_sk} asserts
\[
 NA_1\leq C,qquad
 0\leq p_{\mathrm{RS}}-p_N\leq C/N,
\]
and the replicon limit, but quantifies over a full
\lean{RSSmartPathDisorder}.  It does not convert an arbitrary
\lean{SpinGlass.SKDisorder} into such a path.  A law-preserving product-space
construction and reconciliation of Hamiltonian signs are still needed for
that stronger interpretation.
\end{description}

\section{Recommended completion order}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Give the smart path an explicit finite Gaussian-coordinate model, or
prove a covariance-law theorem strong enough to support differentiation,
concentration, continuity, and cavity decomposition.

\item Prove Gaussian covariance differentiation and then the specialized
identity
\[
 p_N'(s)=\frac{\beta^2}{4}((1-q)^2-A_s).
\]

\item Complete the scalar strict-AT estimates, prove the finite-cascade GT
bound, and construct the Taylor and negative-overlap analytic package.  This
should yield the quadratic constrained-overlap loss
\[
 \mathbb E p_{N,s}^{(2)}(v)
 \leq2p_{\mathrm{RS}}(s)-c_K(v-q)^2.
\]

\item Prove the coupled-pressure envelope and the fixed-deviation exponential
tail using the common Gaussian concentration interface.

\item Strengthen the cavity-interpolation specification with its endpoint
identities, construct the interpolation, and prove
\[
 \|\boldsymbol\rho_s\|
 \leq C_K(N^{-3/2}+T_s).
\]

\item Prove continuity of $D_N$ and the product identity $D_N(0)=0$.
The verified absorption, free-energy, and replicon arguments can then be
assembled without analytic placeholders.

\item If a theorem for the separate concrete SK API is desired, add the
law-preserving model conversion only after the smart-path theorem is
certified.
\end{enumerate}

unproved assumption or an under-specified existence statement;
\item check the paper-to-Lean ledger and high-risk obligations recorded in
Appendix B.36--B.37 of \lean{blueprint_at.aux}.
\end{enumerate}

2--4.  In particular, the public theorems still depend transitively on
\lean{sorryAx}.
