\section{Executive status}

The library target \lean{LatalaMeetsAT} builds successfully with Lean 4.32.1 and
mathlib 4.32.1.  The directory \lean{Lemmas} contains 26 Lean files.  Sixteen
files have no local \lean{sorry}; ten files contain 13 occurrences in total.
There are no project-specific \lean{axiom} declarations and no \lean{admit}s in
this directory.

The build is therefore structurally complete but not certified.  Lean reports
that \lean{SpinGlass.AT.quantitative_strictAT} depends on
\lean{sorryAx}, in addition to the usual foundations
\lean{propext}, \lean{Classical.choice}, and \lean{Quot.sound}.  A file marked
``locally complete'' below has no \lean{sorry} in that file; it may still depend
transitively on an open declaration imported earlier.

Three of the 13 holes are definitions rather than propositions:
\lean{scalarOrderParameter}, \lean{gtSemigroupSolution}, and
\lean{cavityInterpolatedAverage}.  These are foundational interfaces.  Filling
later theorem holes before fixing these interfaces would hide the main analytic
work rather than remove it.

Since the preceding snapshot, the scalar trial identity
\lean{scalarTrialValue_eq} has been proved from a reusable Gaussian convolution
argument.  The proof includes integrability of $\log\cosh$ under Gaussian
measures, rescaling of two independent Gaussian variables, variance addition,
and the endpoint $q=0$.  The target build completes in 3154 jobs.

\section{The 13 remaining sorry obligations}

\subsection{Gaussian interpolation and free-energy derivative}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \lean{GaussianDerivative.lean:quenchedGibbs_deriv_of_covariance_deriv}.
Prove that the explicit \lean{replicaCovarianceOperator} is the derivative of
the quenched finite-replica expectation.  The missing input is smooth
dependence of a finite-dimensional centered Gaussian law on its covariance, or
an explicit affine Gaussian realization with the required domination bounds.

\item \lean{FreeEnergyDerivative.lean:smartPath_freeEnergy_deriv}.  Apply the
finite-Gaussian theorem, prove the diagonal cancellation, and rewrite the
finite replica sums as \lean{overlapSecondMoment}.  The target derivative is
$\frac{\beta^2}{4}((1-q)^2-\mathbb E\langle(R_{12}-q)^2\rangle)$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Scalar Lata\l a comparison}

\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{2}
\item \lean{Scalar/LatalaKernel.lean:latalaF_reference_mean_one}.  Prove
$\int_0^1 F_\lambda(y)/(2\sqrt{1-y})\,dy=1$.  Under $y=1-u^2$, the weighted
inner integral reduces to the antiderivative of
$u/(1+(1-u^2)t)^2$.  The formal proof must justify the endpoint singularity and
the interchange with the Gaussian integral.

\item \lean{Scalar/StrictATSign.lean:scalarOrderParameter}.  Construct
$g_s(u)=\mathbb E[(\partial_x\Psi_s(u,X_u))^2]$ for the local-field diffusion,
including the piecewise dynamics at $q$.

\item \lean{Scalar/StrictATSign.lean:strictAT_sign}.  Below $q$, use the
diffusion derivative and conditional Jensen.  Above $q$, apply the proved
weighted Lata\l a comparison.  Integrate the slope bounds and obtain uniform
local constants by compactness.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Guerra--Talagrand interpolation}

\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{5}
\item \lean{GT/Interpolation.lean:gtSemigroupSolution}.  Define the finite
two-dimensional Parisi recursion for \lean{signedMatrixPath},
\lean{gtMassParameter}, and \lean{gtTerminal}, with its integrability and
endpoint properties.

\item \lean{GT/Interpolation.lean:twoReplica_GT_bound}.  Build the finite
half-mass cascade, identify both endpoints, and prove that the Gaussian
interpolation derivative is nonpositive.  At zero multiplier, prove
factorization into the two scalar RS semigroups and match \lean{gtCorrection}.

\item \lean{GT/Coercivity.lean:gt_quadratic_coercivity}.  Differentiate the GT
functional in its multiplier, combine \lean{strictAT_sign} with a uniform
second-derivative estimate, use compactness away from $q$, and invoke the
proved abstract Taylor-loss lemma near $q$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Coupled pressure and concentration}

\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{8}
\item \lean{CoupledPressure.lean:coupledPressure_sublinear}.  Split over the
$N+1$ attainable overlaps, apply GT coercivity and Gaussian concentration,
then combine the resulting envelope with convexity, \lean{rsGap_deriv}, and
Gronwall to obtain a uniform sequence tending to zero.

\item \lean{FixedDeviation.lean:fixedDeviation}.  Prove the uniform
$O_K(N)$ squared-gradient estimate for the quadratic exponential moment and
apply Gaussian concentration.  This needs an explicit finite Gaussian
coordinate model or a concentration theorem for \lean{RSSmartPathDisorder}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Cavity interpolation and free energy}

\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{10}
\item \lean{Cavity/Interpolation.lean:cavityInterpolatedAverage}.  Define the
last-spin interpolation $\nu_{s,u}(F)$ from an explicit last-spin Gaussian
decomposition, with its normalization, measurability, and endpoint identities.

\item \lean{Cavity/Interpolation.lean:cavity_secondDerivative_bound}.
Differentiate the interpolation twice, classify replica-edge terms using
\lean{Cavity/Coefficients}, and bound the remainder by
$C_K(\operatorname{thirdMoment}+N^{-3})$.

\item \lean{FreeEnergy.lean:rs_freeEnergy_error}.  Prove the product endpoint
identity at $s=0$, extend the open-interval derivative to the closed interval,
and integrate \lean{rsGap_deriv}.  The completed
\lean{uniform_secondMoment} theorem then supplies the $M/N$ upper bound.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Recommended proof roadmap}

\begin{description}
\item[Model and Gaussian API.] Add a finite-index Gaussian-law module.  Decide
whether the current smart-path structure remains law-based or gains an explicit affine
realization.  Finish obligations 1--2 and add endpoint lemmas for
\lean{fullPathHamiltonian}.  This removes the common blocker for free-energy,
pressure, and concentration proofs.

\item[Scalar package.] Finish obligations 3--5.  Gaussian convolution and the
scalar trial identity are complete.  Prove the reference-measure normalization,
then construct the PDE/diffusion order parameter and its uniform strict sign.

\item[GT package.] Finish obligations 6--8.  The recursion should expose its
multiplier derivatives and factorization theorem as named lemmas, since
coercivity uses both.

\item[Pressure and tails.] Finish obligations 9--10.  Reuse the same finite
Gaussian concentration interface created for the smart path, rather than
building a second probabilistic model.

\item[Cavity package.] Finish obligations 11--12 and connect the Taylor
remainder to \lean{cavityRemainder}.  Prove a theorem that constructs
\lean{HasCavityRemainderBound} from the interpolation estimate.  This connection
is currently absent and is necessary to remove the hypothesis from a fully
unconditional version of the main theorem.

\item[Free-energy integration.] Finish obligation 13.  Absorption and its
uniform second-moment estimate are already complete from the explicit
cavity-remainder hypothesis.  The existing \lean{Replicon},
\lean{CompactCorollary}, and \lean{MainResult} proofs should then close by
recompilation once their upstream obligations are filled.

\item[Certification.] Run \lean{lake build LatalaMeetsAT}, search the source for
\lean{sorry}, \lean{admit}, and project-specific \lean{axiom}, and inspect
\lean{#print axioms SpinGlass.AT.quantitative_strictAT}.  Success means that
\lean{sorryAx} is absent.  Ordinary foundations such as choice and quotient
soundness may remain.
\end{description}

\section{Structural issues not counted among the 13 holes}

\begin{itemize}
\item \lean{Cavity/System.lean:cavity_system} is true by unfolding the
definition of \lean{cavityRemainder}.  It does not itself formalize the cavity
expansion.  The analytic content is precisely the unconstructed bound described
above.

\item \lean{MainResult.lean:quantitative_strictAT} assumes the existence of a
constant satisfying \lean{HasCavityRemainderBound data C}.  Thus removing all
13 occurrences is enough to remove the present explicit placeholders, but not
enough to derive that hypothesis from the compact strict-AT assumptions.

\item The free-energy comment saying that the external field is omitted is
stale.  The definition \lean{pathFreeEnergy} now uses
\lean{fullPathHamiltonian}, which adds the deterministic field.  What is still
missing is a proved endpoint identity and the closed-interval calculus needed
to integrate the derivative.

\item \lean{SmartPath.lean} specifies Gaussian marginals and their covariance,
but not a concrete coordinate realization.  This is adequate only after proving
a finite-dimensional Gaussian-law uniqueness and concentration API.  Otherwise
the structure should be strengthened once, near the root of the import spine.
\end{itemize}
